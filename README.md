# CLT Explorer 📊

An interactive way to understand the **Central Limit Theorem** — without getting buried in formulas.

Take samples from different populations, watch their sample means build a distribution, and see how the results connect to the population you're studying.

## 🔎 What can you explore?

CLT Explorer includes three real-world examples:

| Example | Population | Sample Size |
|---|---:|---:|
| 🎣 Fisherman | 100 fish | 10 |
| 🚗 Bangalore Commute | 10,000 commuters | 50 |
| 📱 Phone Usage | 100,000 users | 100 |

Each example lets you repeatedly take samples and watch the **sampling distribution of the sample means** develop.

## 🧠 What you'll see

- **Sampling** — use a smaller group to learn about a larger population.
- **Law of Large Numbers** — see estimates move toward the population average as more data is collected.
- **Central Limit Theorem** — see sample means form an approximately normal distribution as the sample size becomes sufficiently large.

The goal is to make these ideas something you can **see and interact with**, rather than just memorize.
